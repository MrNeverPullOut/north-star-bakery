Our Story

North Star Bakery began as a weekend stall at the local market and grew into the neighborhood shop you see today. We still bake in small batches, just as we did in the beginning.

Our Sourcing Philosophy

We buy flour from regional mills, eggs and dairy from nearby farms, and produce in season. Shorter supply chains mean fresher ingredients and support for our neighbors.
